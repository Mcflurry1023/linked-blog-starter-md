Answer the following. Show your complete solutions. 

1. The sum of the first 20 terms of an arithmetic sequence is 740, and the 20th term is 58. What is the first term of the sequence? 

2. The sum of the first terms of an arithmetic sequence is 960. If the first term is 6 and the common difference is 4, how many terms were added? 

3. An arithmetic sequence has a first term of 11 and a common difference of 5. Which term causes the cumulative sum to first exceed 2,500? 

4. The sum of the first 15 terms of an arithmetic sequence is 375. The sum of the first 30 terms is 1,425. Find the common difference. 

5. The first term of an arithmetic sequence is 8. The sum of the first terms is 1,664, and the last term is four times the first term. How many terms are in the sequence?
