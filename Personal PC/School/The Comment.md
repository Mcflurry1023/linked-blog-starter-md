# SCENE-BY-SCENE PRODUCTION SCRIPT

### **SCENE 1: THE BULLETIN BOARD**

**Location:** Outside School / Main Hallway

**Characters:** Protagonist, Background Students

- **Visual & Action:**
    - Wide shot of students rushing past the bulletin board. The Protagonist stands motionless in front of it, staring at a densely written English poster.
    - Close-up on the Protagonist's eyes widening in confusion.
- **VFX:**
    - **Text Glitch:** The English words on the poster distort, glitching into unreadable symbols and geometric code.
- **SFX:**
    - Sudden audio shift: Normal ambient hallway chatter abruptly cuts into a deep, heavy base vacuum/muffled underwater drone.
    - Sharp school bell ring triggering a fast motion-blur transition.
- **Dialogue / Beat:**
    - **Protagonist:** _"Ha?"_

### **SCENE 2: THE QUICK FIX**

**Location:** Hallway

**Characters:** Protagonist, Friend

- **Visual & Action:**
    - Protagonist turns to his friend, pointing anxiously at the poster for help.
    - Friend casually pulls out his phone, launches the Gemini app, and aims the camera at the board.
    - Protagonist recoils from the screen, stares with a cold, unapproving look, and shoves the phone away.
- **VFX:**
    - **Flashbang Screen:** A sudden blinding white screen flash when the phone turns on.
    - **Scanner HUD:** A bright green laser grid sweeps across the poster with digital floating translation cards snapping into place.
- **SFX:**
    - High-pitched flashbang ring followed by crisp digital camera-scanning sound effects.

### **SCENE 3: THE LECTURE ARENA**

**Location:** Classroom

**Characters:** Protagonist, Teacher, Classmates

- **Visual & Action:**
    - Teacher commands the room like a sergeant, pacing aggressively and slamming chalk onto the blackboard to instil tension.
    - Protagonist slumps in his desk, visibly sweating and anxious.
- **SFX:**
    - A tense, silence-heavy soundscape: Dead room tone broken by sudden, jarring audio hits (loud chalk slams, sharp footsteps, heavy desk creaks).
- **VFX:
    - **Kinetic Shell Attack:** Shouted English words launch off the blackboard like heavy artillery shells, flying straight toward the Protagonist.

### **SCENE 4: INSIDE THE BRAIN**

**Location:** Brain / Mental Space

**Characters:** Protagonist

- **Visual & Action:**
    - Camera zooms tightly into the Protagonist’s eye, transitioning into an abstract mental realm.
    - The Protagonist stumbles across a dark void as loose paper pages fly wildly around him in a storm. He frantically grabs at them, trying to arrange translated sentences in mid-air.
- **VFX:**
    - Swirling vortex of floating text particles and paper assets surrounding the Protagonist.

### **SCENE 5: THE QUESTION & THE MOCKERY**

**Location:** Classroom

**Characters:** Protagonist, Teacher, Classmates

- **Visual & Action:**
    - Protagonist’s hand slowly shakes as it rises into the air.
    - **VFX:** A massive ocean wave rises behind the Teacher, towering over the Protagonist’s solitary desk.
    - Protagonist stammers out his question in English.
    - Teacher stops, peers down coldly, and delivers the line:
        - **Teacher:** _"If you were actually listening, you'd know."_
    - **Time Freeze:** The moment the line finishes, the entire class freezes instantly.
    - Protagonist blinks, completely isolated in the frozen room.
        
- **SFX:*
    - Audio cuts to dead quiet during the freeze.
    - Low, quiet chuckles and whispered snickers ripple through the classroom as motion resumes.
- **Dialogue / Beat:**
    - **Protagonist (whispered):** _"Ha?"_

### **SCENE 6: THE BREAK (THE PARANOIA)**

**Location:** Courtyard / Outside Benches

**Characters:** Protagonist, Friend, Background Students

- **Visual & Action:**
    - Protagonist sits alone on a bench, staring blankly as students walk past.
    - As he zones out, the passing students stop and turn, their faces distorting as they stare directly at him.
- **VFX:**
    - Visual distortion effect: The background warps, and floating spectral silhouettes of laughing faces materialize around the Protagonist.
- **SFX:**
    - Layered, echoing laughter swells in volume, suddenly cut off by a sharp shoulder tap.
- **Action:**
    - Friend snaps him back to reality: _"Hey, come on, we're going to be late for class."_

### **SCENE 7: THE TIMELAPSE (THE SOLITUDE)**

**Location:** Street / Walk Home

**Characters:** Protagonist

- **Visual & Action:**
    - Centered tracking shot: The Protagonist walks in place down the middle of the frame, looking down with a heavy expression.
- **VFX:**
    - **Match-Cut / Seamless Background Cycle:** The Protagonist's walking gait remains constant while the background environment behind him rapidly morphs through changing lighting, weather, and seasonal shifts (sunlight to rain, dusk to night) to visually represent the passage of time.
- **SFX:**
    - Ambient street noises blending seamlessly into a slow, emotional closing musical score.
